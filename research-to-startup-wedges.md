## CROSS-DOMAIN META-WEDGES

These are the themes where multiple agents converged independently — meaning the research base spans domains and the strongest founder picks compound across them.

### M1 — Voice as the new UX layer
*Across: behavioral, AI, health*

84% of Gen Z send voice notes; sub-500ms speech-to-speech latency shipped in 2025 (Deepgram + Groq + ElevenLabs); ChatGPT Voice 900M WAU. Three convergent product opportunities:
- Voice-note-native friend pods (#4)
- Voice-first elderly care companion (AI #4)
- Personal forensics agent over captured audio (#7)

The defensible play is treating voice as the *primary medium*, not a transcription afterthought.

### M2 — Agents as economic actors
*Across: markets, AI*

x402 protocol GA in 2025, GENIUS Act passed July 2025, $280B stablecoin supply. Agents need: identity (Agent KYB), spend authorization (Agent Treasury), reconciliation (GAAP-tagged stablecoin flows), audit trail. Three convergent wedges:
- Agent Treasury (#1) — Ramp for non-humans
- Agent KYB / identity layer for banks (markets #8)
- Vertical agents priced on outcomes (#2, #6, #9)

The compound bet: build the rails AND a flagship vertical that uses them.

### M3 — Late-detected adult conditions = mass premium markets
*Across: health, behavioral*

Three under-served cohorts whose research base just matured:
- Late-diagnosed adult ADHD (CDC: 6% adults, 50% diagnosed in adulthood; women dramatically underdiagnosed)
- Perimenopause/menopause (60-70% gain weight; HRT+GLP-1 synergy *Menopause* 2024)
- Subclinical depression (RESiLIENT trial *Nat Med* 2025, behavioral activation effect size d=1.03)

Common pattern: customer is 35-55, has $150-300/mo to spend, distrusts general healthcare, currently sold nothing actionable. Highest-LTV healthcare segment available.

### M4 — The Mutual cluster (if you want to build something Mutual-shaped)
*Across: matching science, behavioral*

The matching-science agent surfaced 11 findings that directly back a Mutual-style event app. The strongest 4:
1. **Finkel/Eastwick was about romantic chemistry, not professional usefulness** — the "matching algorithms don't work" investor objection is wrong-target. Networking has lower bars and is more like category-fit prediction (which works fine).
2. **Perceived > actual similarity** (Tidwell/Finkel 2013) — the intro-card framing is half the algorithm.
3. **Weak ties are causally validated** (Rajkumar et al. *Science 2022*) — but inverted-U: *moderately* weak ties win.
4. **Deep-level similarity (values/work-style) >> demographic** for pair quality (173-study meta) — directly justifies event-dependent profiling questions, NOT industry/title tags.

Plus: Ingram & Morris (2007 *ASQ*) — at free-form mixers people cluster with existing contacts. The QR-and-deliver-match flow is the *structural intervention* the literature predicts is needed. Plus: Aron 36-questions paradigm + Kardas/Kumar/Epley 2022 (people underestimate how good deep conversations with strangers will be) → ship deeper conversation prompts than feel intuitive.

This is a fully-loaded research base for a defensible product positioning. If Mutual is the thing you actually want to build, the matching-science bonus material is the brief.

## IF I HAD TO BUILD ONE IN 2026

**My top pick across all 65 wedges: Agent Treasury (#1).**

Reason: it's the only wedge on the list that's a *new market shape*, not a better version of an existing market. Every company on earth will have non-human spenders within 3 years; the audit trail and reconciliation layer for that has no incumbent. The Brookings + EY + x402 + Coinbase + GENIUS Act data converge. Closest analog is what Ramp did to corporate cards in 2019, but the multiplier is 100× because agents transact at millisecond cadence and there will be 10-100× more of them than employees. Right founder pair: ex-Ramp/Brex BD + Coinbase x402-protocol engineer. Defensive moat: once you're the audit trail of record, SOX audits and regulator inquiries pull from you — that's a 10-year switching-cost moat.

**My top "if you want to build Mutual itself"**: skip my favorite pick and use the matching-science cluster (M4 above). The research base is *uniquely defensible* — Rajkumar *Science 2022* + Tidwell 2013 + 173-study mentoring meta-analysis + Kardas 2022 + Ingram & Morris 2007. Position as "category-fit and weak-tie engine for events, not romantic chemistry predictor" — that single reframing defuses the Finkel objection that kills every other matching startup pitch.

**My top "outsider bet that could become a $10B company"**: Lithium orotate dementia-prevention brand (#3). The Yankner *Nature 2025* paper is the kind of mechanism-defining finding that gives a consumer brand 5-10 years of narrative tailwind. Molecule is unpatentable (big pharma can't crush you), dose is sub-toxic (supplement + physician oversight, not NDA), APOE4 carriers are a pre-segmented panic-buying market of 80M Americans. Current supplement-aisle lithium orotate is $14 with zero brand. Wrap it like Function Health and you can charge $40/mo with subscription LTV measured in years.
