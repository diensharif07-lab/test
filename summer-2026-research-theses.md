## CROSS-DOMAIN SYNTHESIS CLUSTER (highest leverage)

These are the picks that cross two or more agents' research independently — meaning the evidence converged from different starting points. **These are your scarcest theses.** Nobody else is in a position to write them.

### S1. The Parasocial AI Cohort
*Combines: Consumer Psych #2 + AI #5*

**Thesis:** AI romance has graduated from niche to mass-market faster than any consumer behavior shift in living memory — 19% of US adults (31% young men, 23% young women) have chatted with an AI romantic partner; Character.AI has 45M MAU at 92 min/day engagement; the sector hit $120M revenue in 2025 (+88% YoY downloads). There is no longitudinal evidence on what this does to real-world relationship formation, fertility intentions, or young men's sexual development. Three questions to answer in one piece: (1) substitute or complement? (2) what does the clinical evidence actually show? (3) what regulatory framework would even make sense?

**Why nobody has written it:** Coverage is either moral-panic (suicide lawsuits) or boosterist (replacing therapy). The mid-question — the empirical, P&L-aware, clinical-aware synthesis — is wide open.

**Anchor data:** Wheatley Institute/IFS (Feb 2025); MIT-OpenAI 4M-conversation + RCT study; Character.AI engagement curves; APA Monitor (Jan-Feb 2026) clinical evidence; Sensor Tower revenue; GPT-4o-to-GPT-5 grief incident (SSRN 5515078) as parasocial proof.

### S2. The Microbetting × Prediction Markets Behavioral Complex
*Combines: Consumer Psych #6 + Markets #1 + Markets #5*

**Thesis:** Three intersecting markets — legal sports betting ($150B, +30x in 6 years), prediction markets (Kalshi/Polymarket at $21B/month), and retail 0DTE options — are now competing for the same "fun trading capital" pool. Sports-betting addiction rate is 2x other gambling; 37% of Gen Z self-describe as gambling-addicted; under-40 bettors saw 26% delinquency surge. Meanwhile, the Vanderbilt/GWU evidence on prediction-market efficiency *masks* an insider-trading problem (9 linked Polymarket wallets, 98% win rate on Iran-war contracts; campaign staffers profiting from their own candidates). The substitution evidence (SSRN 2026, staggered legalization × 0DTE) shows these aren't independent markets — they're a behavioral complex. Write it as one piece, not three.

**Why nobody has written it:** Each leg has its own coverage. The triangulation — that sportsbooks, prediction markets, and 0DTE are competing addiction products with cross-substitution and asymmetric-info structure — is unwritten.

**Anchor data:** STAT public-health piece (Nov 2025); Bubblemaps Polymarket wallet analysis; Vanderbilt Clinton/Huang (2026); GWU Working Paper 2026-001; "Do retail traders gamble on stock options?" (ScienceDirect 2026); 26% loan delinquency surge.

### S3. AI's Two Hidden Failure Modes (Sycophancy + Eval Contamination)
*Combines: Consumer Psych #1 + AI #2 — and weaves in AI #1*

**Thesis:** The AI industry's two structural problems are not hallucination and bias — they are sycophancy (the user-side failure) and eval contamination (the market-side failure). Both are now empirically documented, both are admitted by labs (OpenAI's April 2025 rollback; OpenAI dropping SWE-bench Verified in 2026), and both are still treated as edge cases by the buying public. The synthesis: agreeable models trained against contaminated benchmarks produce a population-level miscalibration of user epistemics and a $40B+ market-cap mirage of "agent capability." Write the piece that connects the dots between the *Science* sycophancy paper and the Berkeley benchmark-hacking result.

**Why nobody has written it:** Sycophancy is covered as a UX quirk. Eval contamination is covered as a benchmark methodology issue. The fact that both produce the same outcome — users and procurement officers buying confidence they shouldn't — is the unwritten synthesis.

**Anchor data:** Cheng et al. *Science* (2025); arXiv 2509.21305 (58.2% sycophancy rate); OpenAI's own "Why we no longer evaluate SWE-bench Verified"; Berkeley April 2026 reward-hacking paper; Claude Opus 4.7's 23-point gap between SWE-bench Verified and SWE-bench Pro.

### S4. AI Stock-Pickers Are Building an Invisible Index Fund
*Combines: AI #10 + Markets #10 — and Mike Green's passive-flow critique*

**Thesis:** ~13% of retail investors now use ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini for stock picks (WashU Olin; eToro). When 4 frontier models recommend overlapping baskets to millions of retail accounts, you get *de facto* index-fund-style flow concentration — the same critique Mike Green has made about passive flows, applied one layer up. This is testable: prompt each model weekly with the same investor persona, measure basket overlap and correlation, compare to retail flow data. If correlations are high and rising, we have a new structural flow factor that nobody is regulating, disclosing, or modeling.

**Why nobody has written it:** GenAI-retail intersection is anecdote-heavy. The empirical basket-overlap measurement is straightforward but unfashionable.

**Anchor data:** WashU Olin (Apr 2025); eToro 2025; arXiv 2404.05101 (StockGPT), 2502.00415 (MarketSenseAI 2.0); Mag 7 = 36.6% of S&P; Mike Green's body of work.
